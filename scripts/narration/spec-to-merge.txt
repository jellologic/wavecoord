# Narration for spec-to-merge.mp4. One [section] per chapter (see VIDEOS in scripts/docs_video.py).
# Each sentence is spoken on its own and becomes one subtitle. Keep sentences short.

[intro]
This video follows one wave of work, from a checked spec to merged code.

[plan]
We start from a checked spec tree.
spec waves shows three tasks in the first wave, with no dependencies between them.

[dispatch]
wavecoord dispatch starts the ready wave.
Each task gets a worker on a host, in its own worktree and on its own branch.
The worker holds a lease on the paths its task owns.

[attempts]
attempts ls shows every attempt.
Two have reported, with their turns and their cost.
The index task is still running.

[events]
The lead follows an events feed.
Each line is something the lead may act on.
Here are three reports.
Two are done, and one is blocked on a question about hyphenated words.

[merge]
collect gathers the reports and opens a pull request for each attempt.
Then the refinery merges them one at a time, in dependency order.
The queue store merged.
The index was blocked by the fence.
The tokenizer is blocked because its report was not done.

[gate]
gate-check shows why the index was blocked.
Its branch also changed README.md, a file outside its lease.
The fence keeps that change out of main.

[tree]
Back in the spec tree, the statuses follow the work.
The queue store is done, two tasks wait in review, and the next wave has started.
