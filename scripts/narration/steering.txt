# Narration for steering.mp4. One [section] per chapter (see VIDEOS in scripts/docs_video.py).
# Each sentence is spoken on its own and becomes one subtitle. Keep sentences short.

[intro]
Sometimes a worker needs a nudge while it runs.
This video shows how the lead steers one, without stopping the wave.

[running]
attempts ls, with the active flag, shows the workers in flight.
The replay task is running on the local host.

[hint]
wavecoord hint sends a short message to that worker.
The worker reads it within a few seconds and keeps going.

[model]
steer model switches the worker to another model in the middle of its run.
Here, we move it to opus for a harder part.

[interrupt]
steer interrupt stops the current turn.
The note goes with it, so the worker knows what to do next.

[logs]
The attempt log shows each action arrive.
First the hint, then the model switch and the interrupt, all from the lead.

[feed]
When the worker finishes, its report reaches the lead's events feed, like any other.
The lead collects it and merges it as usual.
